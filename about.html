About Us

Trusted Mosquito Net Solutions Since 1991

Established in 1991, Kundu Traders is a trusted name in mosquito net solutions. Based in Kolkata, we have been serving customers for more than three decades with quality products, dependable service, and competitive prices.

We specialize in mosquito net products and accessories, providing practical and effective solutions for homes, institutions, and commercial establishments. Our goal is to offer products that combine protection, comfort, and durability.

Over the years, we have built long-lasting relationships with our customers through our commitment to quality, honesty, and customer satisfaction. We believe in understanding our customers' needs and providing solutions that meet their expectations.

With more than 30 years of experience, Kundu Traders continues to uphold the values on which the business was founded—integrity, reliability, and excellence. Our dedication to these principles has helped us earn the trust of customers across India.

Whether it is for homes, offices, institutions, or bulk requirements, we strive to provide dependable mosquito net solutions and a satisfying customer experience.
